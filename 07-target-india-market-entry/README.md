# Targeting India: A Strategic Entry Into the Retail Frontier

## Overview
A market-entry strategy analysis exploring Target's opportunity to enter the Indian retail market — covering market sizing, consumer trends, and the competitive landscape.

## Market Opportunity
- India's retail market is roughly $1T today, projected to reach $1.6–1.9T by 2030 and ~$2.8T by the mid-2030s.
- Organized retail is ~18% of the market today, projected to exceed 35% by 2030.
- Online retail is ~$75B (2024), projected to reach ~$260B by 2030 — doubling its share of total retail from 7% to 14%.
- Quick commerce is already a $6–10B GMV market (2024–25), growing 30–40% annually, with potential to double or triple by 2030.
- Retail inflation fell sharply through 2025 (from ~5% to ~0.3% by October), creating a more supportive consumption environment.
- Organized categories like apparel and durables are growing in the low-to-mid teens annually on rising incomes and formalization.

## Competitive Landscape

**Domestic giants**
- Reliance Retail — India's largest retailer, 19k+ outlets across Fresh, Trends, Digital, and JioMart e-commerce
- Spencer's Retail — 120–130 stores across 35 cities, omnichannel with strong private-label offerings
- Croma (Tata) — 550+ consumer electronics stores plus a growing digital presence
- Fabindia — 350+ stores, premium textile/lifestyle positioning built on artisan and heritage products

**International entrants**
- Uniqlo — expanding since 2019, pushing into Tier-2 cities with a cultural-localization strategy
- Other global brands are increasingly exploring or entering the market, adding to competitive intensity
