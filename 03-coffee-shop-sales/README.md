# Coffee Shop Sales — Customer Experience Driven Revenue Risk & Support Optimization

## Core Objective
Identify where customer experience is failing and how it puts revenue at risk, using transaction-level coffee shop sales data.

## Headline Numbers
- Total revenue: $698,812.33
- Total transactions: 214,470

## Key Insights

**Sales by hour**
- Sales peak between 8–10 AM, contributing the largest share of daily revenue — strong morning, commute-driven demand.
- Revenue drops nearly 50% after 10 AM and stays low through the afternoon.
- Evening hours see minimal sales after 7 PM, pointing to underused time slots and potential revenue loss from low engagement.

**Product performance**
- A small set of products — espresso-based drinks and brewed chai — account for a disproportionate share of revenue (concentrated revenue distribution).
- High-volume items like brewed chai and gourmet coffee are core demand drivers and strong candidates for upsell/availability priority.
- Several products contribute negligible revenue despite being carried in inventory — a sign of an inefficient product mix and quiet revenue leakage from underperforming SKUs.

**Day-of-week performance**
- Sales are consistent across the week with no significant weekend spike, unlike typical retail patterns.
- This points to a routine/habitual customer base (e.g. commuters) rather than leisure-driven visits.
- Stable, predictable demand is good news for staffing and inventory planning.

## Recommendations
- Run targeted promotions and demand-stimulation offers during low-performing afternoon/evening slots
- Double down on winning products (espresso, chai, hot chocolate) — promote and bundle with snacks
- Discount, reposition, or cut underperforming SKUs to recover revenue tied up in low-value inventory
- Keep staffing aligned to the stable demand curve rather than over-staffing weekends
- Introduce demand-shaping offers (weekend promos, Friday combos) to create upside where no natural peak exists
- Build loyalty programs around the existing repeat-customer base
